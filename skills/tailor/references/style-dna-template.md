# STYLE-DNA.md template

Copy this structure into the project root as `STYLE-DNA.md`. Keep it short enough to reread before
every page (one to two screens). Every line that states a choice ends with a *because*, pointing at
the story (S), a reference image (R1, R2…) or a dislike (D).

```markdown
# STYLE-DNA — <name of the site>

## The story (in their words)
<3–6 lines quoted or closely paraphrased from the person. Keep their voice.>

## Concept
<One sentence, concrete and visual. Not "modern and clean" — e.g. "A hardware shop's price label:
honest, hand-made, built to last.">

Tension we resolve: <e.g. "old-fashioned but not dusty">

## Audience and job
- Who visits: …
- What they need: …
- Main action: …

## Sources
- S: the story above
- R1: <file or link> — the hook: <what they fell for>
- R2: …
- D: dislikes — <list>

## Colour
| Role | Value | Share | Because |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ground | #… (name) | ~70% | … (R1) |
| Text | #… | | … |
| Accent | #… | ~5% | … |
| (optional) Second ground / muted | #… | | … |
Contrast checked: text on ground x:1, accent on ground x:1.

## Type
- Display: <font> (<fallback>) — because …
- Text: <font> (<fallback>) — because …
- (optional) Utility: … — because …
- Scale & habits: <e.g. "display huge and tight, body 18px, no all-caps labels">

## Composition
<3–5 rules: grid, alignment, density, where the eye goes first, how sections relate.>

## Surface
<texture, borders, corners, shadows (or none), image treatment — with because.>

## Motion
<what moves, when and why — or "almost nothing, because …". Respect reduced motion.>

## Voice
<how the copy talks: 2–3 traits + one sample line in that voice.>

## Signature
<The one or two details someone would remember and that only this site has.>

## Deliberately not
<Things we avoid for this brand, including chosen exceptions to the tell catalog:
"Allowed despite catalog: one-word accent in headlines — because it's the brand's signature (R1).">

## Open questions / placeholders
- …

## Build notes
<plan, decision table, later revisions: date — what changed — why>
```

## Writing tips

- Concrete beats abstract: "newsprint #EDE8DF" says more than "warm off-white".
- Two fonts is usually enough; a third needs a reason.
- The "Signature" section is where uniqueness lives. If it is empty or generic ("bold typography"),
  the site will be forgettable — go back to the story's sensory words and comparisons.
- "Deliberately not" is also where catalog tells become *allowed* when the DNA truly calls for them.
  The critique step will not flag anything listed here as allowed.
